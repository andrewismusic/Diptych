Level is an operator's output when it is heard, and how hard it modulates otherwise. Decay is exponential when short, linear around 4 s, slower to fall when long, and holds at the top. Drag knobs up or down (Shift for fine), double-click to reset.

Keys A to L play; Z and X change octave.
