Scale is shared by both sides; everything in the lower box belongs to the side you pick. Steps are always sixteenths; the rate places notes over them, and a note that runs past the end is cut short. Mixed gives every note its own length. Density thins notes out in an even but irregular order and brings the same ones back as you raise it. The Riff generator builds motifs with answers, pedal-tone riffs, or grouped rhythmic cells, with Syncopation pushing notes off the beat. Notes never overlap: a long note stops where the next one starts. Tap an empty cell to add a note, or drag across to paint several. Tap a note to remove it, or drag it sideways to change its length. Random Notes writes a new melody in the scale, Velocity re-rolls how hard notes play, Rate picks a new rate, Generator picks new generator settings and generates, and All does all of these plus density.
